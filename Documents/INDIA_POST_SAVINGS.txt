INDIA POST SAVINGS SCHEMES 2026 REFERENCE
- Post Office Savings Account: 4.0% interest per annum.
- 5-Year Recurring Deposit (RD): 6.7% interest.
- Post Office Time Deposit (TD): 1 Year (6.9%), 2 Year (7.0%), 3 Year (7.0%), 5 Year (7.5%).
- Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS): 8.2% per annum, 5-year tenure.
- Monthly Income Scheme (MIS): 7.4% per annum, paid monthly.
- National Savings Certificate (NSC): 7.7% compounded annually.
- Public Provident Fund (PPF): 7.1% per annum, EEE tax status, 15-year tenure.
- Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA): 8.2% per annum for the girl child.
- Section 80C Eligibility: NSC, SCSS, PPF, and 5-Year TD qualify for tax deductions.
